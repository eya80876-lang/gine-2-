01

Rhytidectomy
(Facelift)

A comprehensive surgical procedure designed to restore youthful facial contours by addressing sagging skin and deep creases.

How the procedure goes

Incisions are carefully placed along the natural hairline and around the ear. The underlying SMAS layer (muscle and tissue) is tightened, excess skin is removed, and the remaining tissues are smoothly redraped. The procedure typically takes 3-4 hours under general anesthesia.

  • Recovery time: 2-3 weeks
  • Anesthesia: General or IV Sedation
02

Blepharoplasty
(Eyelid Surgery)

Rejuvenates the appearance of the upper and/or lower eyes by excising excess skin and contouring orbital fat.

How the procedure goes

For the upper eyelid, an incision is made in the natural eyelid crease. For the lower eyelid, incisions are made just below the lash line or inside the lid (transconjunctival approach). Excess fat is removed or repositioned, and sagging skin is precisely excised to eliminate "bags" or drooping.

  • Recovery time: 1-2 weeks
  • Anesthesia: Local with Sedation
03

Rhinoplasty
(Nose Reshaping)

Improves facial harmony and proportions by structurally altering the nasal cartilage and bone framework.

How the procedure goes

Performed via a closed (endonasal) or open (trans-columellar) approach. The skin is elevated, and the bone and cartilage are meticulously sculpted to the desired functional and aesthetic outcome before redraping the skin and closing incisions with fine sutures.

  • Recovery time: 1-2 weeks (swelling subsides gradually)
  • Anesthesia: General
04

Augmentation Mammoplasty
(Breast Augmentation)

Enhances breast volume, symmetry, and shape using silicone/saline implants or autologous fat transfer.

How the procedure goes

Incisions are placed in inconspicuous areas (the inframammary fold, periareolar edge, or transaxillary). A surgical pocket is created either behind the breast tissue or under the pectoral muscle, and the chosen implant is securely positioned to achieve a natural contour.

  • Recovery time: 1-2 weeks
  • Anesthesia: General
05

Mastopexy
(Breast Lift)

Elevates and reshapes sagging breasts by removing excess skin and tightening the surrounding tissue.

How the procedure goes

Incisions are tailored to the degree of ptosis (sagging)—often around the areola, vertically down, and along the breast crease. The nipple-areola complex is elevated, breast tissue is reshaped into a tighter cone, and excess skin is excised to create a lifted profile.

06

Reduction Mammoplasty
(Breast Reduction)

Alleviates physical discomfort by removing excess breast tissue, fat, and skin to achieve a proportionate breast size.

How the procedure goes

Through a specialized incision pattern, excess glandular tissue and fat are carefully excised. The remaining tissues are restructured, and the nipple-areola complex is repositioned higher on the chest wall for a lifted, lighter, and more comfortable result.

07

Abdominoplasty
(Tummy Tuck)

Flattens the abdomen by excising excess skin and fat, and repairing separated or weakened abdominal muscles.

How the procedure goes

A horizontal incision is made between the pubic hairline and navel. The skin is lifted, and underlying weakened rectus abdominis muscles are sutured together (fascial plication). Excess skin is removed, and the umbilicus is aesthetically repositioned.

  • Recovery time: 2-4 weeks
  • Anesthesia: General
08

Suction-Assisted Lipectomy
(Liposuction)

Removes localized, stubborn fat deposits to sculpt and refine specific body contours.

How the procedure goes

Tumescent fluid is injected into the targeted area to minimize bleeding and tissue trauma. Small, inconspicuous incisions are made to insert a cannula (a thin hollow tube), which uses a controlled vacuum to gently suction out the displaced fat cells.

09

Brachioplasty
(Arm Lift)

Reduces excess sagging skin and underlying fat from the upper arm region, often resulting from weight loss.

How the procedure goes

An incision is made on the inside or back of the arm, extending from the underarm to just above the elbow. Underlying fat is directly excised or treated with liposuction, and internal tissues are tightened before smoothing the skin over the new contour.
